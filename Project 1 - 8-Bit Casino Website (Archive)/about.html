The Games

Horse Racing

Place your chips on one horse before the starting bell. The horses race across the track, and if yours crosses the finish line first, you win back your bet times that horse's odds.

Slots

Choose your bet and pull the lever. Three reels spin and stop one by one. Line up three matching symbols on the middle row to win; rarer symbols pay more.

Blackjack

You and the dealer each get two cards. Number cards count as their value, face cards count as 10 and aces count as 1 or 11. Hit to take another card or stand to stop. Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over to win.

Roulette

Bet on a single number, a color (red or black) or odd and even, then spin the wheel. If the ball lands on your pick, you win. A single number pays the most because it is the hardest to hit.
