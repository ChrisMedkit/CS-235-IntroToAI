Play Safe

Momentum Tennis is a play-money demo built for a class project. Credits have no cash value, can't be bought, and can't be cashed out. There are no prizes. Nothing here is real gambling, and nothing here is betting advice.

We built it to show how odds are made — and why, over time, the house always comes out ahead.

Healthy habits

  • Treat betting as paid entertainment, not a way to make money.
  • Set a time and money limit before you start, and stop when you reach it.
  • Never chase losses by betting more to win them back.
  • Don't bet when you're stressed, upset or drinking.
  • Keep it to money you can afford to lose — never rent, bills or borrowed money.

Warning signs

  • Thinking about betting most of the time, or needing bigger bets for the same thrill.
  • Hiding betting from family or friends, or lying about losses.
  • Borrowing money or selling things to keep betting.
  • Feeling restless or irritable when you try to cut back.
  • Betting getting in the way of school, work or relationships.

Where to get help

Help is free, confidential and available 24/7:

  • National Problem Gambling Helpline: call or text 1-800-MY-RESET (1-800-697-3738), or chat at ncpgambling.org/chat.
  • Wisconsin Council on Problem Gambling: call 1-800-426-2535 (1-800-GAMBLE-5) or text 850-888-4673. More at wi-problemgamblers.org.

FAQ

Can I cash out my credits?

No. Credits are play money with no cash value. They can't be bought, sold, withdrawn or exchanged for prizes.

Is this real gambling?

No. No real money is involved at any point. It's an educational demo for a college web design project.

Why does the house always win?

Every price includes a margin. A true 50/50 bet pays 1.90 instead of 2.00, and slots pay back about 92 credits per 100 over time. One bet can win, but across many bets the margin adds up. See From probability to odds.

How are the odds made?

A tennis model estimates each player's chance from the points played so far, then a 5% house margin is added. The How the Model Works page walks through it.

I ran out of credits. What now?

Press Reset credits at the top of any page to go back to 1,000. If running out of real money has ever been a problem for you, please reach out to one of the helplines above.

Is "Match Replay" live?

No. It replays real points from the 2023 Wimbledon men's singles, as if they were happening now.
