Notes Scanner - scanner.exe

Turns a photo of your notes into text you can edit, copy or send to the LaTeX editor. Runs in your browser with Tesseract.js, so images never leave your device. Works best on clear, printed or neat handwriting; math symbols usually need fixing by hand.
